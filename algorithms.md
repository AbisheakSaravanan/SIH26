### Random Forest
Looks at different CSI signal features together to identify an activity.
**Example:** CSI changes in a certain pattern → **Walking**

### SVM
Finds boundaries between different movement patterns in the CSI data.
**Example:** Signal pattern A → **Sitting**, Signal pattern B → **Standing**

### CNN
Detects important patterns and sudden changes in the Wi-Fi signal.
**Example:** A sudden signal change → **Possible Fall Pattern**

### LSTM
Studies how the signal changes over time to understand the sequence of movement.
**Example:** Standing → Sudden Movement → Low Movement → **Possible Fall**
